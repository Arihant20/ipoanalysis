⚡ Float Scarcity & Circuit Squeeze Mechanics

When an SME issue has modest retail bidding (1x–4x), retail applicants receive 50%–100% allotment. Post-listing, the entire tradable public float is tiny (often <₹10–15 Cr). High-net-worth investors and momentum desks corner the thin supply, locking the stock into consecutive +20% Upper Circuits with zero sellers.
