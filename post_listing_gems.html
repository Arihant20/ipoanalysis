SECTION 3 • RISK MANAGEMENT

Why 62.4% of Modest Opens Fail • The 4 Fatal Traps & Anchor Unlock Cliff

Identifying the exact structural reasons behind secondary IPO failures and why the SEBI 30-day anchor unlock makes long-term unhedged holding dangerous.

Trap 1: The Discount Open Falling Knife
IPOs opening < -5% suffer a 71.8% failure rate and -13.13% avg return. Discount opens reflect institutional rejection and lack of anchor demand. Rule: Never buy an opening discount.
Trap 2: The Overcrowded Flipper Dump
When sub > 35x opens flat, leveraged retail and HNI flippers panic dump simultaneously: Citichem (410x, crashed -75%), Deccan (102x, crashed -81%), Pranav (126x, crashed -15%). Rule: Max sub ≤ 35x.
Trap 3: Toxic Underwriters & Commodity Traps
Merchant bankers like Khandwala, Horizon, Fast Track Finsec have a 54.4% crash rate. Commodity sectors (foam, textiles, trading, scrap) lack pricing power. Rule: Blacklist enforced.
Trap 4: The 30-Day Anchor Unlock Cliff
50% of anchor shares unlock on Day 30. In our dataset, 44 IPOs surged +35% to +62% in weeks 1-3 (NSDL, BlueStone, Capillary, Oswal Pumps) but bled into losses by Day 35. Rule: Mandatory exit Day 25–27.
