⚖️ Comparative Analysis: Mainboard vs SME Market Dynamics
Key structural and behavioural differences uncovered by tree splits and SHAP values.

🏢 Mainboard Dynamics (Institutional Market)

  • Institutional Anchor Quality: Mainboard listing gains require genuine QIB & Anchor participation. Pure retail hype without institutional support frequently results in post-listing liquidation.
  • Valuation Sensitivity: Higher sensitivity to P/E and PAT margins compared to industry peers. Expensive pricing caps day-1 pop.
  • Liquidity Absorption: Larger float size (avg ₹500 - ₹5,000 Cr) requires hundreds of Crores to move prices, dampening artificial listing squeezes.

🚀 SME Dynamics (Float Squeeze Market)

  • Float Cornering & Bid Capital: total_bid_capital_cr and lm_avg_gain are the #1 drivers. High bid capital on a ₹15-30 Cr issue virtually guarantees upper circuit lock-ins.
  • Lead Manager Dominance: Elite SME lead managers (e.g. GYR, Hem, Horizon) command consistent +40% to +90% listing pops, whereas Tier 4 bankers regularly discount.
  • Demand Density: sub_per_crore strongly predicts multi-day upper circuit continuation and Day 2-15 float cornering.
